* Context
  Discussion regarding additional topics for the "Computational Modeling for Psychology Students" course (Psych 420), extending the current syllabus which covers Turing Machines, I&F Neurons, Hodgkin-Huxley, Perceptrons, Hopfield Nets, and Agent Based Models.

* Proposed Additional Topics
** 1. Reinforcement Learning (The Bridge to Behaviorism)
   - *Rationale:* Connects computational modeling to the history of Psychology (Behaviorism).
   - *Psychological Hook:* Classical Conditioning (Pavlov) and Operant Conditioning (Skinner).
   - *Models:*
     - Rescorla-Wagner Model (mathematically similar to Delta Rule).
     - Temporal Difference (TD) Learning (Dopamine signaling).
   - *Coding:* Multi-armed Bandit or Grid World.
   - *Placement:* After Perceptrons/Delta Rule.

** 2. Decision Making: Drift Diffusion Models (DDM)
   - *Rationale:* Standard model for linking neural firing rates to behavioral RT distributions.
   - *Psychological Hook:* Speed-Accuracy Tradeoff.
   - *Math:* Stochastic Differential Equations (Random Walk/Noisy accumulation).
   - *Coding:* Simulate particle drift to boundary; plot RT histograms.
   - *Placement:* After Differential Equations.

** 3. Bayesian Modeling (Rational Analysis)
   - *Rationale:* Focus on *why* systems work (Marr's Computational Level) vs mechanistic implementation.
   - *Psychological Hook:* Visual illusions (perception as inference), concept learning.
   - *Math:* Bayes' Theorem: \( P(H|D) \propto P(D|H) P(H) \).
   - *Coding:* Coin Flip learner or Cue Combination.
   - *Placement:* Near Theory Creation.

** 4. Symbolic Architectures (Language of Thought)
   - *Rationale:* Contrasts Connectionism; history of Symbolic AI (ACT-R, SOAR).
   - *Psychological Hook:* System 2 processing, algebra, rule-following.
   - *Code:* Context-Free Grammar (CFG) parser in Haskell (recursion).
   - *Placement:* Alongside Turing Machines/Lambda Calculus.

* Deep Dive: Abstract Algebra & Formal Concept Analysis (FCA)
  *User Suggestion:* Introduce Abstract Algebra via Formal Concept Analysis, comparing it to Thagard's Theory of Explanatory Coherence (TEC).

** Pedagogical Strategy
   Use FCA to introduce Abstract Algebra as a tool for *knowledge representation* rather than purely symbolic manipulation.

** The "MTMC" Breakdown
*** 1. Mind: Knowledge Organization
    - *Concept:* Categorization (Rosch), Prototypes vs. Exemplars.
    - *Contrast:* Fuzzy distributed vectors (Neural Nets) vs. Hierarchies/Strict definitions (Logic).

*** 2. Math: Order Theory and Lattices
    - *Formal Context:* Triple $(G, M, I)$ (Objects, Attributes, Relation).
    - *Algebra:* Galois Connections.
      - Derivation operators mapping sets of objects to shared attributes and vice versa.
    - *Structure:* Concept Lattices.

*** 3. Theory: The "Battle of the Models"
    Compare **FCA** against **Thagard's Explanatory Coherence (TEC)**.
    | Feature | Thagard's TEC | Formal Concept Analysis |
    |---------+---------------+-------------------------|
    | Type | Connectionist / Constraint Satisfaction | Algebraic / Structural |
    | Logic | Probabilistic, "Soft", Dynamic | Deterministic, "Hard", Static |
    | Mechanism | Energy minimization / Settling | Mathematical Closure |

    *Assignment Idea:*
    1. Run a dataset (e.g., medical diagnosis) through a TEC simulation (Hopfield-style).
    2. Run the same data through FCA to generate a Lattice.
    3. Discuss: Which explains the data better? Does the lattice reveal structural implications the network obscured?

*** 4. Code: Haskell Implementation
    Haskell is ideal for Set/List processing required for FCA.
    - *Task:* Implement naive closure algorithms.
    - *Visualization:* Output to Graphviz (dot) to draw Hasse Diagrams.

** Strategic Value
   - Introduces **Ontologies** and **Knowledge Graphs** (Semantic Web, RAG).
