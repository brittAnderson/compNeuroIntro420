Notes for classroom session on Mind and Brain Section

* Goal
  I want the students to both review their basic neurophysiology knowledge, and to start to wrestle with some of the abstract ideas that we will encounter.

* My Objectives
  1. What is the action potential and how does it come about.
  2. What are the basic anatomical constituents of a neuron?
     - meant to get at axons, dendrites and the like.
  3. What is a brain?
     - Intended to set up some questions about whether a computer can think?
     - Or how central is a "brain" to being a thinking being?
